# OptiRewards 💳🍎
### The Smart Apple-First Credit Card Reward Optimizer

> **Never leave money on the table.** OptiRewards recommends the optimal credit card from your personal wallet the second you walk into a store or check out online.

---

## 1. Executive Overview

OptiRewards is designed specifically for the Apple ecosystem (iOS, Apple Watch, and macOS). It solves a universal consumer dilemma: modern cardholders carry multiple credit cards (cash back, travel points, rotating quarterly categories, store co-brands), each with distinct multipliers, spend caps, and point valuations. 

OptiRewards detects where you are standing or what you are buying online, cross-references your active wallet, and tells you the exact card to swipe or tap with Apple Pay in under one second.

### 2. Real Geolocation & Nearby Store Detection

### Does it actually grab my real physical location?
**Yes!** OptiRewards includes real GPS location integration directly in the browser:
* Tap **"Use My GPS"** and the browser will securely request your location.
* The app connects in real-time to the **OpenStreetMap Overpass API**, querying physical businesses within a 500-meter radius of your coordinates.
* It instantly lists the real supermarkets, coffee shops, and stores on your street, along with their distances.

### Do we actually *need* a visual map?
**A visual map is intentionally omitted for speed.** 
At the physical checkout counter, a user has 2 to 3 seconds before paying. Opening a full-screen map, waiting for tiles to load, and zooming in creates unnecessary friction.
* **The OptiRewards Solution:** On app open, GPS queries nearby businesses. The closest stores appear as 1-tap quick buttons.
* **Granular Category Fallback:** If GPS is disabled, signal is lost, or a store is unlisted, you can instantly tap one of the granular category chips (*Fast Food*, *Supermarket*, *Wholesale Clubs*, etc.).

## 5. Database-Driven Category Taxonomy

Rather than artificial micro-splits or bundling unrelated spend, categories are aligned with real credit card reward MCCs:
- 🍽️ **Dining & Restaurants:** Unifies all restaurants, cafes, fast food, coffee, bars, and food delivery under one MCC-aligned reward tier.
- 🛒 **Groceries & Supermarkets:** Supermarkets, neighborhood grocery stores, farmers markets (excluding wholesale/supercenters).
- ⛽ **Gas Stations & EV Charging:** Gasoline service stations, fuel pumps, EV charging networks (Tesla, EVgo, Electrify America).
- ✈️ **Flights & Airfare:** Separated from hotels! Directly targets airline ticket purchases (Amex Plat 5x, Sapphire 3x).
- 🏨 **Hotels & Lodging:** Separated from flights! Directly targets hotel stays, resorts, Airbnb, and boutique lodges.
- 🚆 **Transit & Commuting:** Subways, buses, trains, ferries, rideshare (Uber/Lyft), parking, and tolls.
- 💊 **Drugstores & Pharmacies:** Stand-alone pharmacies (CVS, Walgreens, Rite Aid).

- 🔨 **Home Improvement:** The Home Depot, Lowe's, Ace Hardware.
- 📱 **Phone & Internet:** AT&T, Verizon, T-Mobile, Comcast/Xfinity, broadband utilities.
- 🎬 **Streaming & Subscriptions:** Netflix, Disney+, Hulu, Spotify, Apple Music.

## 7. Running the App

Since OptiRewards is a 100% serverless static application, there are no build steps or backend servers required.

1. **Local Testing:** You can open `public/index.html` directly in your browser, or spin up a simple local server:
   ```bash
   cd public
   python3 -m http.server 8085
   ```
   Then open **`http://localhost:8085`** in your browser.

2. **Production:** Simply deploy the `public/` directory to any static hosting provider (e.g., Cloudflare Pages, GitHub Pages, Vercel).


## 8. Customizable User Point Valuations

Every credit card points enthusiast values points differently:
- **Cash Back purists** may value points strictly at **1.0¢/pt**.
- **Hyatt / Airline transfer optimizers** value Chase Ultimate Rewards and Bilt at **2.0¢–2.5¢/pt**.
- **Delta / SkyMiles flyers** value SkyMiles at **1.2¢–1.4¢/pt**.

OptiRewards now includes a dedicated **"Point Values"** interface:
- **Interactive Sliders:** Adjust valuations in real-time from `1.0¢` to `2.5¢` for Ultimate Rewards, Membership Rewards, Capital One Miles, ThankYou Points, Bilt, United, Delta, AA, Marriott, and Hilton.
- **One-Tap Presets:**
  - 🪙 **Cash Only:** Flattens all points to 1.0¢ (straight cash-back comparison).
  - ✈️ **Standard Travel:** Uses conservative industry averages (1.5¢–1.8¢).
  - 👑 **Max Transfer Yield:** Optimizes for high-value transfer partners (2.0¢–2.2¢).
- **Live Re-calculation:** When you adjust any slider, card yields and top recommendation rankings update immediately without reloading.

## 10. Active-Card Point Valuation Filtering
The **Point Values** tab automatically inspects your active wallet:
- Only currencies corresponding to cards **actively enabled in your wallet** are shown (e.g. if you only have Chase Sapphire and Amex Gold active, only *Ultimate Rewards* and *Membership Rewards* appear).
- Next to each slider, the app displays which of your specific active cards use that point currency.
- If all your active cards earn direct cash back, an informative card confirms: *"All cards in your active wallet earn direct cash back (fixed at 1.0¢ = $0.01)."*
